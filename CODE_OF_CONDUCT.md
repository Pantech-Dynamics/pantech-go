# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
We want everyone who takes part, whether by opening issues, reviewing or contributing code, to
have a respectful, harassment-free experience.

In short: be welcoming and kind, respect differing views, accept constructive feedback gracefully,
and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing
others' private information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through
[pantechdynamics.com/contact](https://pantechdynamics.com/contact).
All reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.
